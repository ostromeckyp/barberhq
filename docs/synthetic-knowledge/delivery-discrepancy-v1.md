# Missing or incorrect products in a delivery

**SYNTHETIC DEMO PROCEDURE — invented for DevFest, not an actual Not Bad Stuff policy.**

## missing — Fewer items than expected

Compare the delivery reference and expected quantity with the items actually present. Record expected and received quantities separately, with the salon and product identifier. An absent item is a delivery discrepancy, not a damaged item. Ask the HQ delivery coordinator to investigate; do not confirm receipt of units that are not present.

## wrong-product — A different product arrived

Record both the requested product and the product actually delivered, including codes when available. Ask HQ to resolve the discrepancy before accepting it as a substitute. Similar names or styling effects do not establish that two products are interchangeable.

For example, Pomada Matowa and Pomada Dry Matt are distinct catalog products. If the delivered item is also visibly damaged, consult DAM-001 as well. Do not infer a replacement entitlement or change inventory from a chat answer. The demo assistant provides instructions only; discrepancy resolution is outside the implemented replenishment happy path.
