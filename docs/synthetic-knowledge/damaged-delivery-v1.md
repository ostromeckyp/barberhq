# Damaged products in a salon delivery

**SYNTHETIC DEMO PROCEDURE — invented for DevFest, not an actual Not Bad Stuff policy.**

## intake — Identify the affected items

Use this procedure when a delivered product has visible damage, such as a cracked container, damaged closure or leaking contents. Identify the affected product and quantity separately from intact items in the delivery. Do not mark an affected unit as received in saleable stock merely because the parcel arrived. Set the affected items aside from stock intended for sale or salon use and ask HQ for further handling instructions.

This procedure does not specify spill cleanup, chemical exposure, disinfection or disposal methods. For those questions, use the relevant approved safety instructions; this demo document cannot supply them.

## report — Prepare a damage report for HQ

Record the salon, delivery or transfer reference, product name and code, number of affected units and a short description of the visible damage. Include the batch identifier if it is readable; if not, record that it is unavailable. Include photos of the affected product and relevant packaging when these can be taken safely, avoiding people and customer information.

Send the report through the salon's established internal reporting channel to the HQ delivery coordinator. This synthetic procedure does not specify a real email address or external service. Retain the delivery reference and report details so HQ can relate its decision to the affected items.

## decision — Await the HQ disposition

HQ reviews the report and tells the salon whether a replacement, return or other resolution is appropriate. A damage report does not automatically authorize replacement stock, a refund, a carrier claim or disposal. Do not change inventory merely because the assistant displayed these instructions.

Any eventual replacement or inventory correction must use the appropriate separate, authorized workflow. The DevFest knowledge-assistance use case ends with guidance and sources; it does not submit the report or execute those workflows.
