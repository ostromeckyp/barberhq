# Requesting and confirming routine replenishment

**SYNTHETIC DEMO PROCEDURE — invented for DevFest, not an actual Not Bad Stuff policy.**

## request — Specify the product and unit quantity

Select the salon and product, then request an explicit number of individual retail units. Two means two additional units, not a target total of two and not two cartons. If the product or quantity is unclear, clarify it before submission. This demo permits single-unit transfers; this is not a statement about actual wholesale case sizes.

## movement — Approval, dispatch and receipt

HQ approval reserves the requested units. HQ confirms dispatch after handing over the goods, reducing warehouse on-hand stock and releasing the corresponding reservation. The destination salon confirms receipt of the intact quantity actually received; this increases its on-hand stock and closes the transfer when all dispatched units are accounted for.

The demo's ordinary transfer uses exact full quantities at each step. A missing, wrong or damaged delivery is not an intact full receipt: stop that confirmation and consult the applicable guidance. The separate knowledge-assistance example does not alter the ordinary transfer's completed record.
